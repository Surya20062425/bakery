Our Story

From Our Kitchen to Your Home

We are located inside More Supermarket — Palvoncha, serving Gandhi Nagar, KTPS, and all of Palwancha since 2015.

Every morning, we bake in small batches. Real butter. Fresh cream. Premium dry fruits. No preservatives, no premixes, no shortcuts. Whether it is a last-minute loaf of bread or a three-tier wedding cake, we treat it with the same care.

Our sweets follow family recipes — the kind of Mysore Pak and Kaju Katli that taste like they came from a home kitchen, because they essentially did.
